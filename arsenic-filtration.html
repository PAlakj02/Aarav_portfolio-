Arsenic and Heavy Metal Filtration

Low Cost Portable Device to filter Arsenic and other heavy metals.
Design Patent Application no-202531130896.

Innovation

During my internship with Prof. Robin Dutta (Tezpur University, Assam) I studied Arsenic- remediation chemistry and understood the feasibility of arsenic filtration and the on ground challenges with the existing methods. These solutions were expensive or difficult to implement.
We designed a low-cost handheld portable bottle that integrates Arsiron Nilogon method with a novel piston-driven filtration mechanism. The process uses oxidation-coagulation-adsorption (OCOP) method. It can be used by defence personnel, students, and travellers operating in arsenic or heavy metal-affected areas.

Community

Automated Community System for Arsenic and heavy metal filtration

Developed an automated community system using tank-level sensors, a programmable logic controller, point-to-point IoT communication, and three calibrated chemical-dosing pumps. Used a chemically resistant six-armed polypropylene stirrer.

Partnered with the Government of Assam- to distributed the hand held bottles and the community filter. Impacting approximately 1200 families

Tested results from 20 different tube wells showed that arsenic had reduced by 97.8%, Iron by 94.4% and turbidity by 96.8%- well below WHO drinking water guidelines.
