Arsenic and Heavy Metal Filtration

Low Cost Portable Device to filter Arsenic and other heavy metals.
Design Patent application no-202531130896.

My grandparent’s hometown – Assam is known as the cancer capital of India. In the villages I came across people with skin lesions, patchy skin, lung diseases and some even had gangrene.
On enquiring further, I found out that the ground water has beyond permissible levels of arsenic and other heavy metals like iron and lead in water.
During my internship with Prof. Robin Dutta (Tezpur University, Assam) I studied Arsenic- remediation chemistry and understood the feasibility of arsenic filtration and the on ground challenges with the existing methods. These solutions were expensive or difficult to implement.

We designed a low-cost handheld portable bottle that integrates Arsiron Nilogon method with a novel piston-driven filtration mechanism. The process uses oxidation-coagulation-adsorption (OCOP) method. It can be used by defence personnel, students, and travellers operating in arsenic or heavy metal-affected or remote areas.

We tested of the bottles with water from 20 different tube wells. The results showed that arsenic had reduced by 97.8%, Iron by 94.4% and turbidity by 96.8%- well below WHO drinking water guidelines.

Community Impact

Automated Community System for Arsenic and heavy metal filtration

Developed an automated community system using tank-level sensors, a programmable logic controller, point-to-point IoT communication, and three calibrated chemical-dosing pumps. Built a chemically resistant six-armed polypropylene stirrer and added a worm-gear gearbox to reduce speed from 960 to approximately 50 rpm while increasing torque.

Partnered with the Government of Assam- to distributed the hand held bottles and the community filter. Impacting approximately 1200 families
