STEM in Action

I imparted STEM knowledge by conducting classes for the underserved students over a span of 1 year. Taught concepts like buoyancy, aerodynamics, water cycle, energy etc with hands on science experiment kits. Empowered more than 150 students with the knowledge and practical application.

Developed the curriculum and authored a book- “The Science That Wins Matches” that explains science concepts through different games like projectile motion with basketball, Newton's Law of motion with football, Magnus effect with cricket etc. Expanded the concept in over three states impacting around 10,000 students.

Developed a mentor mentee model where I trained 16 student mentors who subsequently went on to train 250+ students. Organised a science exhibition along with the student mentors with 300+ student visitors.
