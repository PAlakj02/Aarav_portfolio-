Innovation

It started as a project at Plaksha summer school in May 2024, converted into something more extensive. This project aims to design and build an affordable, automated irrigation system that intelligently dispenses water based on soil moisture levels through IoT. Using Arduino and ESP32 microcontrollers, combined with a soil moisture sensor and water pump, the system monitors soil dryness and activates watering mechanisms accordingly.

By combining automation with ease of use, the system offers a practical model for modernizing traditional agricultural practices at the grassroots level. A low-cost irrigation solution that can assist small-scale farmers in water conservation and efficient crop maintenance, demonstrating that simple, accessible technology can meaningfully reduce farmer workload and water wastage.

Community Impact

Collaborated with the Govt. of Assam to deploy the device across 20 farms across Titabor (Assam).
Partnered with the Indigo Knowledge Prism Foundation to establish a village research-lab initiative and trained 15 children from farming families to build, test, and maintain devices independently.
The system has been installed in 35 farms across Assam and Haryana.

Commercial Impact

Developed a Business Plan to commercialise the manufacturing of the product with appropriate pricing and distribution network. Detailed a roadmap to build the device for commercial manufacturing.
