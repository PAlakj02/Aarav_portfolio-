GOLD Crest Award for Innovation and Sustainability

Innovation

This project aims to design and build an affordable, automated irrigation system that intelligently dispenses water based on soil moisture levels through IoT. Using Arduino and ESP32 microcontrollers, combined with a soil moisture sensor and water pump, the system monitors soil dryness and activates watering mechanisms accordingly.

Community Impact

Installed the system in 35 farms across Assam and Haryana

Collaborated with the Govt. Of Assam to deploy the device across 20 farm across Titabor (Assam)
Partnered with Indigo Knowledge Prism Foundation to establish a village research-lab initiative, trained 15 children from farming families to build, test, and maintain devices independently. It gave the children confidence to work with sensors, coding and build an entire automated system independently and contribute to their family’s welfare.

Impact and Sustainability
  • 20% reduction in water consumption
  • 30% increase in plant growth yield
  • Low cost
  • Saves farmers time and effort
  • Easy to install
  • Real time update

Commercial Impact

Developed a Business Plan and a construction manual to commercialise the manufacturing for a company that makes other irrigation equipment. Established an appropriate price and distribution model for the device.
Sold the construction model for INR 70,000 with a commitment to provide assistance in the formal adoption in the product catalog.
