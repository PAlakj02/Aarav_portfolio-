Innovation

It started as a project at Plaksha summer school in May 2024, converted into something more extensive. This project aims to design and build an affordable, automated irrigation system that intelligently dispenses water based on soil moisture levels through IoT. Using Arduino and ESP32 microcontrollers, combined with a soil moisture sensor and water pump, the system monitors soil dryness and activates watering mechanisms accordingly.

Data reading at 15 farms over 10 days

Impact and Sustainability
  • Low cost
  • Saves water
  • Increases crop yield
  • Saves farmers time and effort
  • Easy to install
  • Real time update

Community Impact

Installed the system in 35 farms across Assam and Haryana

Collaborated with the Govt. Of Assam to deploy the device across 20 farm across Titabor (Assam)
Partnered with the Indigo Knowledge Prism Foundation to establish a village research-lab initiative and trained 15 children from farming families to build, test, and maintain devices independently. It gave them confidence to work with sensors, coding and build an entire automated system besides giving them a sense of accomplishment as they could contribute to their family welfare.

Commercial Impact

Developed a Business Plan and a construction manual to commercialise the manufacturing for a company that makes other irrigation equipment. Established an appropriate price and distribution model for the device.
Sold the construction model for INR 70,000 with a commitment to provide assistance in the formal adoption in the product catalog.
